# About Timotheus Gmeiner

Timotheus Gmeiner is a product strategist, applied scientist, and inventor. He helps innovative leaders de-risk, prototype, and position frontier technologies. His work delivers productized hardware and software automations, defensible intellectual property, and enterprise strategies that bring applied science to market.

Strategies that build trust. Prototypes that prove value. Stories that move people. Countless prototypes. Multiple products. One exit. He currently serves as Product Lead at Autodesk, integrating AI into automotive design software (Alias and VRED). The 3D GenAI startup he led, BlankAI, was acquired by Autodesk.

He works with executive leadership and innovation teams across startups, research labs, and global enterprises. Clients and partners have included Worldcoin, Richemont, Nike, Autodesk, Siemens Energy, Continental, RocketLab(SI), Chipcare Corporation, Ventripoint Diagnostics, Rolls-Royce Aero, Morgan Solar, BionikLabs, Synaptive Medical, Nia Technologies, Christian Blind Mission, University of Toronto.

The public record includes 29 documented engagements, 11 patents across medical devices, surgical simulation, MRI calibration, and generative AI, and 4 publications (SIGGRAPH, ISMRM, IAC, NVIDIA). Further practice notes live at https://www.buildwonder.co.
